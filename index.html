About Me

Hello! I'm Darien, a Computer Science student at Cal State East Bay with a 4.0 GPA, graduating in May 2027. I presently work on campus as a Supplemental Instruction Leader where I support 21 students in CS 101, design weekly lesson plans, and run 90-minute active learning sessions. Prior, I worked as a Learning Assistant and tutored 4th-6th grade math one-on-one, which influenced my eventual mastery of patience and communication. I learn programming best through a hands-on approach. Recently, I developed a large Minecraft Forge 1.12.2 backport of Immersive Enchanting, working through custom interfaces, progression systems, compatibility, and release. My skills and experience then culminated into my most recent project, Catenna, where I developed a multi-agent pipeline that coordinates Codex, Claude, and AGY throughout software development. Currently, I have a strong skillset of Python, C++, Java, React, Linux, and Git, and am actively developing my experience with AI, ML, and software engineering. Overall, my most ambitious goal is to further expand Catenna and contribute towards reliable AI systems that can make complex technical work more effective and accessible.
